# The Promptor positioning and homepage milestone

## Goal
Make the product identity unmistakable and show, within the first screen, why The Promptor is more useful than a blank chatbot.

## Brand hierarchy
- Make **The Promptor** the primary customer-facing product name.
- Use **by Blacure** as the consistent parent-brand endorsement beside or beneath it.
- Keep Blacure on existing downloadable prompt-pack names, where it already identifies the publisher.
- Replace conflicting labels such as “Blacure — AI Song Prompt Generator,” “Open Generator,” and mixed product references across the homepage and primary navigation.

## Homepage changes
- Rebuild the opening section around:
  - **THE PROMPTOR**
  - **by Blacure**
  - **AI Music Prompt Studio**
  - A concise promise centered on building production-ready AI song blueprints.
- Keep the strongest low-friction offer prominent: **10 free prompts. No signup. No credit card.**
- Use one clear primary action: **Build Your First Blueprint**.
- Add an immediate before/after demonstration:
  - “What you say” — a short creative idea.
  - “What The Promptor builds” — a detailed production-ready example covering tempo, key, instrumentation, vocals, arrangement, and mix character.
- Add a concise “Why not just use ChatGPT?” comparison showing structured producer controls, consistent output, and music-focused guidance without making unsupported claims.
- Reframe existing features around the production workflow rather than generic AI generation.
- Present the existing monetization ladder clearly: 10 free prompts, $5 for 20 prompts, $19.99/month unlimited Standard + Pro Studio, and separate $2 downloadable packs.
- Preserve the existing comment form and functional links.

## Consistency pass
- Update visible brand labels on the generator, lyrics, pricing, sign-in, and account surfaces to follow **The Promptor by Blacure**.
- Update page titles, descriptions, social titles, site name, and organization/site descriptions to match the new hierarchy.
- Keep each route’s current purpose, pricing behavior, authentication, generation logic, credits, and checkout unchanged.
- Keep the current logo and dark gold visual system, while tightening typography, spacing, and mobile stacking for a more established studio-product feel.

## Validation
- Check the homepage at mobile and desktop widths for readable copy, clean button wrapping, and no overlap.
- Verify all homepage links and calls to action reach the intended existing pages.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors and that generation, sign-in, and payment behavior remain untouched.

## Not included in this milestone
Prompt DNA, cloud project libraries, artist profiles, version history, Suno/Udio optimization modes, new legal pages, and new subscription features remain future milestones.
